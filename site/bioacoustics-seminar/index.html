PAST EVENT · 16 JUNE 2026 · MAASTRICHT

Bioacoustics
Student Seminar

A one-day hybrid research seminar with students and researchers from Maastricht, Utrecht, and Tilburg universities.

DATE16 June 2026
TIME10:30–16:00 CEST
VENUERoom C2.007, Maastricht

PROGRAMME

Talks & abstracts

Select a talk for a short summary. The original event page has the full abstracts.

10:30 Welcome & opening · Aki Härmä
SESSION 1 · ACOUSTIC MODELING & SEQUENCE ANALYSIS
10:40Casper BröchelerEvent-Based Causal Analysis of Zoo Aviary Soundscapes Using Point Process Models＋

Acoustic and environmental events are modeled as time series to explore how earlier events relate to later bird vocal activity.

11:10Bernardo Amado Pereira da CostaLanguage-Model-Based Sequence Modeling for Bird Vocalizations＋

Compares sequence models and language-model approaches for learning temporal structure in bird vocalization events.

11:40Daniel MarconTransformers for Deinterleaving Symbol Sequences in Bird Vocalizations＋

Investigates transformer models for separating overlapping vocalization sequences into their likely sources.

12:10 Lunch break
SESSION 2 · POPULATION & INDIVIDUAL-LEVEL ANALYSIS
13:10Gaspar KuperDiscovery and Identification of Bird Individuals Using Acoustic and Proximity Data＋

Combines sound, proximity signals, and observations to study individual Rüppell’s vultures.

13:40Emre ArgınAvian Population Estimation from Acoustic Recordings＋

Examines detection features, acoustic embeddings, and regression for bird population estimation in zoo aviaries.

14:10 Coffee break
SESSION 3 · VOCAL COMPLEXITY & COMPARATIVE BIOACOUSTICS
14:40Céline AngoninComparing Multi-Task Strategies for Multiple Dataset Learning in Bioacoustics＋

Studies whether training across different bioacoustics datasets improves classification and generalization.

15:10Thijs DuinmeijerComparing Vocal Complexity of Old World Vultures＋

Tests relationships between social complexity and vocal repertoire across vulture species.

15:40 Open questions & closing
